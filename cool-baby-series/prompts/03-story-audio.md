# Story, music and narration prompts

## Script + shot list (Claude / ChatGPT)
```
You are the head writer of a premium preschool animated series (Bluey-level warmth and comic timing, but original).

SERIES: "Cool Baby" (Hebrew: "הבוסית במשקפיים") – [BABY], a ~10-month-old baby who doesn't talk yet but is the calmest little boss in the world, and her two giant Akita dogs: [DOG1] (pink collar – goofy, over-excited, always smiling) and [DOG2] (black collar – serious, careful, secretly soft). RULE OF THE SHOW: when [BABY] puts on her bear-ear sunglasses, the ordinary world turns into a big imaginary adventure; when she takes them off, we are back in reality and the "huge" problem turns out to be tiny.

FORMAT: horizontal 16:9 episode, 3:15–3:45 total.
Structure: COLD OPEN (0:00–0:20, a funny real-life moment) → TITLE (8s theme) → ACT 1 (problem appears, sunglasses go on) → ACT 2 (adventure in the imaginary world: obstacle, failed attempt, the baby's clever idea) → ACT 3 (resolution, sunglasses off, the reveal that it was small) → TAG (a last gag) → END CARD.
45–60 shots, 2–6 seconds each. No character dialogue: the baby communicates with expressions and gestures, the dogs with dog behavior. One Hebrew narrator (the dad), warm and funny, 12–18 short lines total, never explaining what we can already see.

EPISODE IDEA: [paste the client's chosen plot / real moment]

WRITE:
1. Title (Hebrew + English) and logline.
2. Beat outline with timecodes.
3. Shot list table: #, timecode, duration, shot size & camera, location + angle, characters (max 2 moving), action (max 2 beats), continuity notes (props/hat/positions), narration line (Hebrew or "—"), SFX, music cue.
4. Visual comedy through reactions and timing; include at least one running gag that can recur in future episodes.
5. Safety: no scary moments, no danger to the baby near water/heights, no unsafe behavior a toddler could copy.
6. A YouTube title, description (Hebrew + English) and 3 thumbnail ideas.
```

## Theme song (Suno — paid plan only)
```
Style: playful ukulele and xylophone kids theme, light bossa groove, finger snaps, 105 bpm, warm and cool at the same time, catchy, Hebrew vocals.

Lyrics (8–10 seconds):
[Intro]
משקפיים על האף…
[Hook]
היא הבוסית! (ווף ווף!)
הבוסית במשקפיים!
```
For a serious series, consider commissioning a human composer for the theme (ownership + Content ID safety).

## Underscore
```
Instrumental. Playful adventurous kids underscore, pizzicato strings, marimba, soft jungle percussion, builds gently then resolves, 90 seconds, no vocals, leaves space for a narrator voice.
```
