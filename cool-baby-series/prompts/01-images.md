## 2. Character sheet — the girl
Attach: 4–6 real photos (at least one without sunglasses) + approved style frame.
```
Using the attached photos of the same baby girl and the attached approved style frame, create a professional CHARACTER MODEL SHEET for an animated series.

STYLE: {STYLE}

CHARACTER: {BABY_DNA}

LAYOUT (wide landscape sheet, plain light-gray background, even soft lighting, small English labels only):
Row 1 – full body turnaround, sitting cross-legged: FRONT, 3/4 LEFT, SIDE PROFILE, BACK. Also CRAWLING side view.
Row 2 – head close-ups, 6 expressions: calm cool boss, big laugh, surprised, curious, sleepy, determined.
Row 3 – accessories: sunglasses on, sunglasses slid down the nose (eyes peeking over), sunglasses off, with gingham hat, without hat.

RULES: identical proportions, colors and outfit in every view. Keep her face recognizable (round face, full cheeks, dark eyes, dark side fringe) but stylized. No extra accessories, no text besides labels.
```

## 5. Locations — run each separately
```

[YARD_JUNGLE] The SAME backyard transformed into a magical jungle as seen through a baby's imagination: the turf becomes giant soft grass, the white walls become tall pale cliffs with vines, the sandstone rock becomes a small mountain, huge friendly flowers, glowing warm light.
```
